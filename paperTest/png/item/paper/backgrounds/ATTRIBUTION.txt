Paper background textures in this folder are rendered PNG exports of SVG assets
copied from the StarHorizon repository (Space Station 14 fork):
https://github.com/ (StarHorizon), Resources/Textures/Interface/Paper/

Files:
- paper_background_default.png
- paper_background_book.png
- paper_background_dotmatrix.png
- paper_background_perforated.png
- paper_background_blood_red.png
- paper_content_lined.png
- paper_content_dotmatrix.png

See StarHorizon's Resources/Textures/Interface/Paper/attributions.yml and its
repository license files (LICENSE.TXT / LICENSE-AGPLv3.txt) for asset licensing terms.

Note: paper_background_perforated.png and paper_background_dotmatrix.png are
UI reading-window backgrounds (PaperVisuals.backgroundImagePath) for
PaperNanoTaskItem/ForensicReportPaper and PaperArtifactAnalyzer respectively -
distinct from those prototypes' WORLD-sprite states (paper_receipt*/
paper_dotmatrix*), which live in the parent png/item/paper/ folder instead.
